What these modes mean
  • Original only: the post's own sentiment (same as the chart above, 100+ posts only).
  • Reply sentiment: a hollow dot at the replies' average sentiment (or engagement-weighted average if the checkbox is on), next to the original's solid dot.
  • Delta: an arrow from the original's sentiment to the replies'. Left-pointing red arrows mean the crowd was more negative than the poster.
  • Agreement rate (key metric): share of replies that agree vs. disagree. Color shows agreement among opinionated replies (agree ÷ (agree+disagree)): green = crowd backs the poster, red = crowd pushes back. X = disagree rate. For neutral news posts, agree = approving replies, disagree = skeptical replies.
  • Combined conversation: one number for the whole thread: the original weighted by its interactions plus every reply weighted by its own engagement.
  • Topic drift: share of replies whose topic differs from the post's (including "other"). Dots recolor by drift; the sankey shows where the conversation went.

How to read this

Sentiment runs from −1 (clearly negative about Anthropic or its IPO) to +1 (clearly positive). Posts between −0.15 and +0.15 count as neutral, which is mostly news relayed without opinion.

Interactions = likes + reposts + replies + quotes. The weighted average counts each post in proportion to its interactions, so it shows what most people actually saw. The plain average treats every post equally.

Topics are the single main subject of each post. Start with the default (posts with 100+ interactions), then drag the slider to 0 to see the long tail.

Main pattern: the biggest accounts mostly relayed neutral news. The negative tone comes from the S‑1's own safety risk warnings, founder control, and losses versus a ~$2T valuation. Revenue growth is the only topic that leans positive, and even it is contested.

Methodology & caveats

Data from X search, read-only. Built as a static site; data in data/posts.json.
